Xi (Veleska) Chen

I am an M.A. Economics student at Columbia University, interested in behavioral economics, microeconomic theory, organizational behavior, and psychology. I study how people make decisions under rules, incentives, and institutional constraints: how they interpret expectations, navigate strategic environments, and judge others' behavior in organizations.

I am especially interested in bringing formal models closer to the texture of human behavior. My work draws on economic theory, experimental design, and NLP/text-as-data methods to turn ambiguous social behavior into structured, testable questions. I am currently preparing for Ph.D. applications and welcome conversations with researchers, students, and potential collaborators with related interests.
