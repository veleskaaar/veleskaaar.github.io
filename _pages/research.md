My research experience spans behavioral economics, organizational behavior, labor and aging, consumer behavior, and computational social science. I am particularly drawn to questions about how people make decisions under institutional constraints, uncertainty, and technological change.

Selected Research Experience
======

**Reference Points and Value Uncertainty**  
Incoming Summer Research Assistant, Columbia University Economics Department  
Supervised by Prof. Mark Dean, June 2026-August 2026

* Will contribute to an ongoing paper on reference points and value uncertainty, with a focus on theoretical refinement and experimental design.

**Letter-Spirit Divergence and Strategic Compliance in Institutional Settings**  
Research Assistant, Columbia Business School, Morris Lab  
Supervised by Prof. Michael Morris, January 2026-Present

* Contributed to a coauthored working paper on malicious compliance and strategic rule adherence, focusing on divergence between the letter and spirit of rules in organizational contexts.
* Helped design and implement a chatbot-based negotiation experiment to study how negotiation behavior differs when participants unknowingly interact with AI versus human counterparts.

**Organizational and Institutional Research Projects**  
Research Assistant, Columbia Business School  
Supervised by Prof. Lori Yue, January 2026-Present

* Supported a firm-level project on nationalism and patriotism, examining how these orientations are expressed in organizational narratives and corporate behavior.
* Conducted literature review, text data collection and coding, and NLP-based semantic analysis of firm communications to support early-stage research development.

**The Reemployment Process and Experience of Older Workers**  
Researcher, Suzhou, China, November 2024-December 2025

* Designed a structured evidence-review protocol on older-worker reemployment, screening 21,987 Web of Science records and refining the final sample to 168 studies.
* Built a bibliometric and thematic mapping framework to characterize core debates, methodological patterns, and open research gaps.
* Contributed to multiple rounds of revision and resubmission for a coauthored paper currently under review at the Academy of Management Annual Meeting.

**Psychological Resilience in Work-Family Conflict Among Accompanying Mothers**  
Capstone Project, Suzhou, China, November 2024-May 2025

* Conducted semi-structured interviews with 16 mothers to collect primary data on work-family conflict and psychological resilience.
* Used Python tools including spaCy, Sentence Transformers, VADER, and TextBlob for interview analysis, sentiment polarity measurement, and topic distribution visualization.

**Human-Computer Interaction Features of Smart Wearable Devices and Purchase Intentions**  
Project Director, Soochow University, May 2023-May 2025  
Supervised by Prof. Dian Zhu and Prof. Yi Shen

* Conducted bibliometric analysis of customer preference using CiteSpace.
* Designed a discrete choice experiment with SPSS orthogonal analysis and deployed the survey on Credamo.
* Analyzed trade-offs among HCI features using a sample of 380 respondents and estimated consumer preferences with binary logit models in Python and Stata.

**Corporate Behaviors and Employee Performance**  
Independent Researcher, UC Berkeley, January 2024-May 2024  
Supervised by Prof. Benjamin R. Handel

* Used secondary data and multivariate regression analysis to examine the relationship between wage strategies, workplace psychology, and employee attrition.
* Applied the Entropy Weight Method to construct psychological measures and developed a related working paper.

**Digital Touchpoints and Consumer Behavior in the Metaverse**  
Independent Researcher, March 2023-September 2023  
Supervised by Prof. Aleksei Parakhonyak
