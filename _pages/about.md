I am a Master of Arts student in Economics at Columbia University. My research interests sit at the intersection of behavioral and organizational economics, institutional design, labor and aging, and computational social science.

I am especially interested in how people and organizations respond to rules, incentives, uncertainty, and new technologies. My recent work uses a mix of theory-building, experimental design, survey methods, text analysis, and NLP-based tools.

Research Interests
======

* Behavioral and organizational economics
* Institutional rules, strategic compliance, and rule interpretation
* Labor, aging, and older-worker reemployment
* Consumer behavior, human-computer interaction, and technology adoption
* NLP, text analysis, experimental design, and applied microeconomic methods

Current Work
======

I am currently working as a Research Assistant at Columbia Business School on projects related to malicious compliance, negotiation behavior, nationalism and patriotism in firm communications, and organization-level narrative analysis.

In Summer 2026, I will join Columbia University's Economics Department as a Summer Research Assistant, contributing to research on reference points and value uncertainty.
